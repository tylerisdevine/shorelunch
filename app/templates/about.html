About ShoreLunch

ShoreLunch is a personal recipe management application designed to help you organize, discover, and cook your favorite meals.

The name comes from the tradition of "shore lunch" - cooking freshly caught fish right on the shoreline. It's about simple, satisfying food made with care.

Features

  • Recipe Management - Add, edit, and organize your recipes
  • Quick Save - Save a recipe with just a name, fill in details later
  • Favorites - Mark your favorite recipes for quick access
  • Want to Cook - Keep a list of recipes you want to try
  • Tags - Organize recipes with custom tags
  • Ratings & Comments - Rate your recipes and add notes
  • Search - Find recipes by name, ingredients, or tags
  • Photo Upload - Add photos to your recipes

Getting Started

Start by adding a new recipe. You can add as much or as little detail as you want - even just a recipe name to fill in later!

Open Source

ShoreLunch is open source and available on GitHub. Contributions and feedback are welcome!
